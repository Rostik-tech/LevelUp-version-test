Privacy Policy

Last updated: March 2026

1. Introduction

Welcome to Level Up Gaming. We respect your privacy and are committed to protecting your personal data. This Privacy Policy explains how we collect, use, store, and protect your personal information when you visit our website, create an account, or purchase our products.

Level Up Gaming operates an online store offering branded gaming mousepads and related accessories. When you use our website, certain personal data may be collected in accordance with applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

For the purposes of applicable data protection legislation, Level Up Gaming acts as the data controller responsible for processing your personal data collected through this website.

2. Information We Collect

We may collect different types of information when you use our website, create an account, place an order, or interact with our services.

  • Account Information: Name, email address, username, and other details you provide when creating an account.
  • Shipping Information: Delivery address, phone number, postal code, and other details required to deliver your order.
  • Payment Information: Payment details required to complete your purchase.
  • Order Information: Products purchased, order history, and transaction details.
  • Technical Information: IP address, browser type, device information and usage data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use the personal information we collect for various purposes related to operating our website, processing orders, and improving our services.

  • Process and fulfill your orders.
  • Send order confirmations and shipping updates.
  • Provide customer support and respond to inquiries.
  • Improve our website and services.
  • Analyze website usage and trends.
  • Send promotional offers (with your consent).

Our processing of personal data is based on applicable legal grounds under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR).

4. Payment Processing

Payments made through our website are processed securely by third-party payment service providers. Level Up Gaming does not collect or store your full payment card details.

When you make a purchase, your payment information is transmitted directly to the payment provider and processed according to their privacy policies and security standards.

Payment service providers may collect and process personal and payment information in order to complete transactions, prevent fraud, and comply with legal obligations.

Secure Payment Processing

Payment providers use industry-standard encryption and security technologies to protect your payment information during transactions.

5. Data Security

We implement appropriate technical and organizational security measures designed to protect your personal data from unauthorized access, disclosure, alteration, or destruction.

SSL Encryption

All data transmitted between your browser and our servers is encrypted using secure technologies.

Secure Storage

Personal data is stored on secure systems with restricted access.

Access Control

Only authorized personnel have access to personal information.

Security Monitoring

We regularly review and update our security practices.

While we take reasonable steps to protect your personal information, no system can guarantee complete security.

6. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Our website uses cookies, local storage, and similar technologies to improve your browsing experience, provide essential website functionality, and analyze how our website is used.

Cookies are small data files stored on your device when you visit a website.

  • Essential cookies: Required for the basic functionality of the website.
  • Preference cookies: Remember your language and currency settings.
  • Session technologies maintain your shopping cart and login session.
  • Analytics technologies help us improve website performance.

You can control or disable cookies through your browser settings.

7. Your Rights

Under applicable data protection laws, including the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR), you have several rights regarding your personal data.

Right of Access

Request a copy of your personal data.

Right to Rectification

Update or correct your information.

Right to Erasure

Request deletion of your personal data.

Restrict Processing

Restrict how your data is processed.

Data Portability

Receive your data in a portable format.

Right to Object

Object to certain types of data processing.

Withdraw Consent

Withdraw your consent at any time.

Lodge a Complaint

File a complaint with a data protection authority.
